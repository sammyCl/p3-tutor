# Mission: Adjusting new kitten to apartment

## Why 
I am getting a new kitten in a week. I have never had a kitten before, so I am unsure what the process is like for getting them adjusted to my apartment. I want help laying out a plan so I know what to get for the kitten before I bring them home, how to handle their first couple days at my apartment, and how I should get them adjusted to my apartment.

## Success looks like
- I have developed a list of things to buy and setup before my kitten's arrival.
- I can help my kitten get comfortable with me during the first couple days in my apartment by getting them used to my scent, voice, and by playing with them.
- I know how to introduce my kitten to the rest of my apartment.

## Constraints
- I have never had a kitten before, and I don't know what they need.
- I have 1 week before I get my kitten.

## Out of scope
- Any type of veterinary appointment.
- Specific food, litter, or other cat item brands.
- How to introduce the kitten to other animals.
- How to take care of specific cat breeds.
